# Community Conduct

This project welcomes teachers, students, developers, translators and researchers from different backgrounds.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, and give constructive feedback. Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, deliberate humiliation and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that seriously disrupt the community or place participants at risk. If a problem occurs, open a private security/contact channel with the maintainer rather than escalating it publicly when that would expose personal information.

The goal is a collaborative project where people can learn, contribute and disagree professionally.
